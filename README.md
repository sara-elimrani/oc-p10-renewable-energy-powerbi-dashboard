# Renewable Energy Performance Dashboard

This project was completed as part of the OpenClassrooms Data Analyst training program.

The objective was to design an interactive Power BI dashboard for monitoring renewable energy production and performance in France, while comparing France with other European countries.

---

## Project Context

The energy transition requires reliable indicators to identify regional dynamics, measure renewable energy performance, and understand France's position within Europe.

The dashboard combines French regional production data, performance indicators, European renewable energy usage, and dynamic RTE Eco2mix data.

---

## Objectives

- Track renewable energy production by French region and energy source
- Analyze capacity factors and coverage rates
- Compare renewable energy usage across European countries and sectors
- Position France within the European energy transition
- Provide an interactive decision-support dashboard

---

## Data Sources and Scope

- French regional renewable energy production: hydro, solar, wind, bioenergy, and renewable gas
- French performance indicators: capacity factor and coverage rate
- European renewable energy shares: electricity, transport, heating and cooling, and overall usage
- RTE Eco2mix API data: consumption, production, energy mix, and forecasts

---

## Tools and Technologies

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Python for data preparation
- RTE Eco2mix API

---

## Data Preparation and Modeling

- Removal of unused columns
- Missing-value handling
- Standardization of dates, regions, and energy types
- Creation of dimension tables
- Separate preparation of French production data and European usage data
- Constellation-style data model for cross-analysis

---

## Dashboard Scope

- Regional renewable energy production in France
- Performance by energy source and region
- European renewable energy benchmark
- France's position by sector
- Dynamic monitoring of the French electricity mix
